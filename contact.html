FAQ

Quick answers.

The questions we hear most before a first conversation.

What hardware does Federated.One need?

A single 2U GPU server, in the rack you already have — no separate facility and no farm of racks to build.

Do we need to buy more GPUs?

No. Federated gets more from the GPUs you already have — bigger models, longer conversations and more concurrent users.

How does Federated handle governance and compliance?

Tenants are kept separate, security is built into every layer, every request is logged for audit, guardrails protect against unsafe use, and cost is tracked automatically.

Who is Federated.One for?

Five kinds of clients: governments, banks, enterprises, ISVs and token factories. See how each one uses it.

What AI apps are included?

A private marketplace with finance, knowledge base, HR and BI apps ready to go, plus room for your own tools and approved outside AI.

What is the AI Landlord model?

The partner owns the infrastructure — a real, durable asset. Others rent space on it, and pay for it, again and again. Learn about partnering.
