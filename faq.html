Frequently asked questions

About FederatedOne, its four layers, security and governance, and working with us.

Topics 01General 02Platform 03Compute 04Security and governance 05Partners 06Working with us

01General

What is FederatedOne?

One accountable integrator for private AI, from software to data centre. It brings together four layers that work together or stand alone, for clients and partners.

What are the four layers?

Four layers, from top to bottom. Layer 1: FederatedOne Beads & EcoPartners, a build team for any agentic tool and a trusted network that bridges partners and clients. Layer 2: FederatedOne Agentis, the complete AI stack and agent platform. Layer 3: FederatedOne Compute, private GPU servers with enterprise NVIDIA GPUs. Layer 4: FederatedOne DataCenter, the data centre for your private cloud or on-premises build.

Can we take just one component?

Yes. Layers can be combined or taken one at a time.

Who powers FederatedOne?

FederatedOne is powered by Evvo Group.

02Platform

What does FederatedOne Agentis include?

A complete AI stack, from local model serving to enterprise integrations and the agent platform. It is multi-tenant out of the box, with security and governance built in.

Where does FederatedOne Agentis run?

On-premises, inside your own network, on FederatedOne Compute GPU servers.

What can the research capability produce?

Cited research across large document sets, with output in Word, PDF, PowerPoint and Excel.

Can Agentis run agents and routines?

Yes. Agents, routines and a meeting notetaker, all with human approval gates.

What can FederatedOne Beads & EcoPartners build?

A build team realises any agentic tool you need, on top of Agentis. Examples include tender and bid-response assistants and technical documentation search. Tools are integrated with your systems, under the same approval gates and audit trail.

What does FederatedOne DataCenter provide?

The data centre offering to run your private cloud or on-premises build: hosting, power, cooling, networking and security, under your control.

03Compute

What is FederatedOne Compute?

Private GPU servers with enterprise NVIDIA GPUs, run in your own data centre.

Where do the GPUs run?

In your own data centre, housed in FederatedOne DataCenter and kept under your control.

Can business units share the GPUs?

Yes. GPU capacity is sized to the workload and shared across business units.

Which GPUs does FederatedOne Compute use?

Enterprise NVIDIA GPUs in your own data centre, sized to the workload and shared across business units.

04Security and governance

Does our data stay in our network?

FederatedOne Agentis runs on-premises, inside your own network. FederatedOne Compute uses GPUs in your own data centre, and FederatedOne DataCenter keeps the facility under your control.

How do we stay in control of what agents do?

Agents and routines operate under human approval gates, with role-based access, metering and an audit trail.

Which standards is it mapped to?

Role-based access, metering and the audit trail are mapped to ISO 27001, the GDPR and the EU AI Act.

Is the platform multi-tenant?

Yes. FederatedOne Agentis is multi-tenant out of the box, with security and governance built in.

05Partners

How does the EcoPartner network work?

It bridges partners and clients: a trusted network of vendors, integrators and software builders for clients, and access to clients and a governed platform for partners.

What do partners get?

Access to FederatedOne clients and a governed platform to build on, under the same approval gates and audit trail.

How do we become a partner?

Send us an enquiry and choose “Partner” on the contact form.
