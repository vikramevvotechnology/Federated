Frequently asked questions

About FederatedOne, its five components, aiDAPTIV+, security and governance, and working with us.

Topics 01General 02Platform 03Compute and aiDAPTIV+ 04Security and governance 05Partners 06Working with us

01General

What is FederatedOne?

One accountable integrator for private AI, from software to data centre. It brings together five components that work together or stand alone, for clients and partners.

What are the five components?

Federated Agentis (A), the complete AI stack and agent platform; Federated Build (B), a build team for any agentic tool; Federated Cloud (C), GPU servers with MiPhi aiDAPTIV+ built in; Federated DC (D), the data centre for your private cloud or on-premises build; and Federated Ecosystem (E), a trusted network that bridges partners and clients.

Can we take just one component?

Yes. Components can be combined or taken one at a time.

Who powers FederatedOne?

FederatedOne is powered by MiPhi aiDAPTIV+, MiPhi Syntax and Evvo Group.

02Platform

What does Federated Agentis include?

A complete AI stack, from KV-cache offload to local model serving, enterprise integrations and the agent platform. It is multi-tenant out of the box, with security and governance built in.

What is Syntax on AgentStation?

Syntax runs on AgentStation (Pro Suite), on an on-premises appliance inside your own network, as part of Federated Agentis.

What can the research capability produce?

Cited research across large document sets, with output in Word, PDF, PowerPoint and Excel.

Can Agentis run agents and routines?

Yes. Agents, routines and a meeting notetaker, all with human approval gates.

What does Federated Build do?

A build team realises any agentic tool you need, on top of Agentis. Examples include tender and bid-response assistants and technical documentation search. Tools are integrated with your systems, under the same approval gates and audit trail.

What does Federated DC provide?

The data centre offering to run your private cloud or on-premises build: hosting, power, cooling, networking and security, under your control.

03Compute and aiDAPTIV+

What is aiDAPTIV+?

MiPhi's memory extension, built into Federated Cloud GPU servers. It extends GPU memory using flash inside the box.

What happens when a session outgrows GPU memory?

It spills onto in-box flash instead of failing. aiDAPTIV+ Link adds capacity and aiDAPTIV+ Cache keeps it responsive.

What are the benefits?

Larger models, longer context and more users on the same GPUs. Gains depend on workload and configuration.

Which GPUs does Federated Cloud use?

Enterprise NVIDIA GPUs in your own data centre, sized to the workload and shared across business units.

04Security and governance

Does our data stay in our network?

Syntax on AgentStation runs on an on-premises appliance inside your own network. Federated Cloud uses GPUs in your own data centre, and Federated DC keeps the facility under your control.

How do we stay in control of what agents do?

Agents and routines operate under human approval gates, with role-based access, metering and an audit trail.

Which standards is it mapped to?

Role-based access, metering and the audit trail are mapped to ISO 27001, the GDPR and the EU AI Act.

Is the platform multi-tenant?

Yes. Federated Agentis is multi-tenant out of the box, with security and governance built in.

05Partners

What is Federated Ecosystem?

It bridges partners and clients: a trusted network of vendors, integrators and software builders for clients, and access to clients and a governed platform for partners.

What do partners get?

Access to FederatedOne clients and a governed platform to build on, under the same approval gates and audit trail.

How do we become a partner?
