How it works

From your rack to running AI.

Four simple stages take an organization from the hardware it already has to a governed AI platform its teams — and its customers — can use.

  1. 01

    Install

    Federated runs on a single 2U GPU server, in the rack you already have.

  2. 02

    Run

    Run bigger models, remember longer conversations and serve more concurrent users — without buying more GPUs.

  3. 03

    Govern

    Tenants kept separate, every request logged, guardrails in place and cost tracked automatically.
