What is FederatedOne?

One accountable integrator for private AI, from software to data centre. It has four layers (Beads & EcoPartners, Agentis, Compute and DataCenter) that work together or stand alone.

Can we take just one component?

Yes. Layers can be combined or taken one at a time.

What is FederatedOne Compute?

Private GPU servers with enterprise NVIDIA GPUs, sized to the workload, run in your own data centre and shared across business units.

Where does it run?

FederatedOne Agentis runs on-premises, inside your own network. FederatedOne Compute uses enterprise NVIDIA GPUs in your own data centre, and FederatedOne DataCenter provides hosting, power, cooling, networking and security under your control.

How is governance handled?

Role-based access, metering and an audit trail, mapped to ISO 27001, GDPR and the EU AI Act, with human approval gates for agents and routines.

How do partners work with FederatedOne?

FederatedOne Beads & EcoPartners gives partners access to clients and a governed platform, and gives clients a trusted network of vendors, integrators and software builders.
