01What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. Similar technologies, such as local storage, work in a comparable way. They can be used to make a website work, to remember your choices or to understand how a website is used.

02Cookies we use

This website does not currently set any cookies of its own, and it does not use analytics or advertising cookies. It works fully without them.

If we introduce cookies in future, for example to understand how the site is used, we will update this table and, where required, ask for your consent first.

03Managing your preferences

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Most browsers let you block cookies altogether, or only from certain websites. Because this website does not rely on cookies, blocking them will not affect how it works.

04Third-party services

This website loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests them, Google receives technical information such as your IP address. Google Fonts does not set cookies. You can read more in Google’s own privacy information.

Documents we link to, such as our one-pager, open directly from this website.

05Changes to this policy

We will update this policy if the way this website uses cookies changes. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last updated.
